CHAPTER ONE · THE PHYSICS

Fusion, explained visually.

Begin with the reaction, then follow the energy through the plasma, magnets, vessel and the eventual power system.

THE CORE REACTION

Small nuclei. Enormous energy.

Fusion joins two light nuclei into a heavier one. The deuterium–tritium reaction creates a helium nucleus, a neutron and 17.6 MeV of energy.

In a hot plasma, charged particles can be guided by magnetic fields. The neutron is electrically neutral and escapes those fields, transferring much of its energy to surrounding materials.

Deuterium + Tritium → Helium + neutron
THE BIG IDEA

Four links in the energy chain

01 / FuelHydrogen isotopes

Deuterium and tritium supply the reactants.

02 / ConditionsHot, dense plasma

Particles must collide enough to fuse.

03 / ConfinementHold the fuel

Magnetic fields or rapid compression retain energy.

04 / ConversionCapture the heat

Future plants would turn captured energy into electricity.
